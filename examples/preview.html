Cells, chains and chasers emerge on their own.

Simple rules, living patterns

Each particle only feels its neighbours: types attract or repel each other with their own strengths. Nothing else is programmed, yet clusters form, chase one another and split again.

A background, not a show

The simulation pauses when it scrolls out of view or the tab is hidden, and draws a still frame when the reader prefers reduced motion.
